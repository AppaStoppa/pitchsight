From a clip to a coaching report

Computer vision does the counting. A vision model does the coaching. Four steps, run in this order.

1

Track

Every player and the ball get an ID that follows them through the clip.

2

Split the sides

Kit colours, or which side of the net they stand on, put every player on one side.

3

Read the shape

Players cluster by depth into lines, bands or rings: a 4-3-3 in soccer, a 3-2 in basketball, a 3-3 in volleyball.

4

Write the report

A vision model studies key frames and the movement data, then rates and coaches every player.

PitchSight

Built for Hacklanta with YOLOv8, OpenCV and Claude. Positions are estimated from the video, not measured on the playing surface, and the report is a model's judgement, not ground truth.

Privacy: no account and no sign-in. A clip you upload is processed on the server and deleted within 24 hours. Clips you save to My clips stay in your own browser only. When the AI report is on, a few still frames from the clip are sent to Claude (Anthropic) to write it.

Uploading…

A deep scan checks every frame for every player and the ball, so it takes one to three minutes. You can keep this tab open.

Annotated clip

Every tracked player gets a ring under their feet in their team colour, and the ball is the glowing yellow ring with a trail (dashed when it is estimated between sightings). Select a player to see a white dashed ring (ideal position) and an orange arrow (the run to make).
